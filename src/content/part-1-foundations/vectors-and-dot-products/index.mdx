import { Hook, Intuition, Formalize, Play, WorkedExample, Steps, Step, Variables, Variable, Summary } from "@/components/chapter/Beats";
import { IntuitionDemo, PlayDemo, DotProductCheckpoint } from "./Demos";

<Hook>
  How do you measure whether two directions roughly agree, roughly oppose, or have nothing to do with each other?
</Hook>

<Intuition>
  <IntuitionDemo />

  A vector is just an arrow from the origin. Drag $b$ around $a$ and watch the angle $\theta$ between them — that angle alone already tells you "agree," "oppose," or "unrelated."
</Intuition>

<Formalize>
  The **dot product** turns that agreement into a single number —

  $$
  a \cdot b = a_x b_x + a_y b_y
  $$

  <Variables>
    <Variable>**$a$, $b$** — the two vectors being compared.</Variable>
    <Variable>**$a_x, a_y$** — the components of vector $a$.</Variable>
    <Variable>**$b_x, b_y$** — the components of vector $b$.</Variable>
    <Variable>**$a \cdot b$** — the dot product itself: a single number measuring alignment.</Variable>
  </Variables>

  <Steps>
    <Step label="An equivalent, angle-based form">
      The dot product is exactly equal to $|a|\,|b|\cos\theta$.
    </Step>
    <Step label="Read the sign">
      Its sign tells the story:
      - positive when $\theta < 90°$ (roughly the same direction)
      - zero at exactly $90°$ (perpendicular)
      - negative beyond that (roughly opposite)
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Formalize>

<Play>
  <PlayDemo />

  Drag $b$ and watch $a\cdot b$ and $\theta$ move together — the dot product crosses zero at exactly the moment the arrows form a right angle.
</Play>

<WorkedExample>
  With $a=(4,0)$ and $b=(3,4)$:

  <Steps>
    <Step label="Compute the dot product">
      $$
      a\cdot b = 4(3) + 0(4) = 12
      $$
    </Step>
    <Step label="Compute each magnitude">
      $$
      |a|=4, \qquad |b|=5
      $$
    </Step>
    <Step label="Solve for the angle">
      $$
      \cos\theta = \frac{12}{4\times5} = 0.6 \implies \theta \approx 53.13°
      $$

      Comfortably under $90°$, matching a positive dot product.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</WorkedExample>

<Summary>
  $$
  a \cdot b = a_x b_x + a_y b_y = |a|\,|b|\cos\theta
  $$

  The dot product measures alignment: positive means pointing roughly together, zero means perpendicular, negative means pointing roughly apart.
</Summary>
